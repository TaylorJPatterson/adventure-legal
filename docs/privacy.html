Privacy Policy

Adventure: Reawakened · Last updated: August 4, 2026

Adventure: Reawakened does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

The app runs entirely on your device. It makes no network connections. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or any other personal information.

Purchases

The app offers a single one-time in-app purchase that unlocks the full game. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive or store your payment information. Apple's handling of purchase data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Because the app collects no data, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
