Restoring your purchase

The full game is unlocked with a single one-time purchase. To restore it on a new device, or after reinstalling, open the app, go to the unlock screen, and tap Restore Purchase. The unlock is tied to the Apple ID you bought it with.

Playing offline

The app needs no account and no internet connection. Everything runs on your device.

Frequently asked
